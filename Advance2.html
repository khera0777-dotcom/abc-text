Daily Attendance

Click P, A or L for the Morning and Evening shift. Type an optional remark and an optional advance salary amount given on this date. Press Enter or click away to save a remark or advance. Click the same P/A/L button again to clear it.

Monthly Report

Salary rule: Each shift (Morning, Evening) counts as half a day.
Daily pay = Monthly salary ÷ calendar days in that month. Pay per shift = Daily pay ÷ 2.
Deduction = Pay per shift × Absent shifts.   Estimated payable = Monthly salary − Deduction.
Balance to Pay = Estimated payable − Total advance given in that month.
Leave (L) is paid. Shifts with no entry are NOT deducted. Amounts are rounded to 2 decimals.
These are estimates only, not final payroll. Check them before paying anyone.

Backup and Restore

Important: Your data is stored inside this Chrome browser on this PC only. It is not on the internet or in the cloud, and it does not copy itself to other devices. Clearing Chrome's browsing data can delete it. Make a backup regularly and keep the file somewhere safe (for example a USB drive).

Restoring replaces the current data, but only after you confirm. A safety copy of your current data is kept in the browser first.
